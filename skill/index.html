Two things to know before you use it

It will not invent a number, and it will tell you so

Every figure comes from one dated file. Ask for a slide about pricing, or for a metric nobody has signed off, and the slide comes back with a visible ⟦FACT NEEDED⟧ marker instead of a plausible figure.

That is deliberate. A hole you can see is recoverable; a confident wrong number in front of a client is not. Pricing and legal disclaimers are not in the file yet — expect the marker on those subjects and come to the brand team.

A JPG is frozen the day it is made

Every figure on it was true on the date stamped in the footer, and nowhere else. If you build a deck now and present it in three months, check the numbers again before you do.

If one is wrong, don't fix it in your slide — tell us, we fix it at the source, and it becomes right in everyone's deck.

Checking a deck you already have

The same pack audits existing decks for terminology, unsourced claims, banned words and figures that do not match the record. Paste the deck's text and ask it to run the audit — CHECK.md has the procedure.
